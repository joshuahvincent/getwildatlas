Why families trust it

Wild Atlas is a narrated, documentary-style animal encyclopedia for ages 3–9: 200+ animals across 13 packs (3 always free), read aloud in 5 languages. Founded by Josh Vincent, who grew up in a conservation documentary filmmaking family. 10% of proceeds are pledged to conservation. We just launched, so we’re not bringing you traffic — we’re giving your families something trustworthy to take home.

How redemption works

Apple offer codes: one custom code per venue (e.g. RIVERBEND), redeemed in one tap by link or the app’s “Redeem a Code” sheet. Member-exclusive, with a redemption cap and optional expiry. Nothing to build on your side.

The co-branded book

Your hero animals in connect-the-dots, mazes and spot-the-difference pages, your logo on the cover, and a “claim your free pack” page in the back. Pre-reader friendly, ages 3–9. See the example at wildatlasapp.com/partners.
