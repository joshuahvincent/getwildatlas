Design notes

  • Home order follows your brief: hero and rails, My Packs, a dashed rule, My Places, a second rule, then Explore More Animal Worlds and the one peachPop CTA (DESIGN_SYSTEM §7 Home; peachPop on one action only, §1).
  • My Places reuses the pack tile idiom (pastel gradient, soft thick bottom edge) with the venue emblem as the art, a NEW! capsule (GameDenCardBadge look) and meta pills. The pin glyph and the dashed "Add a place" tile carry meaning without reading (NN-4, TC-1).
  • Pack page keeps the canon: back button, tinted hero header, Meet the Animals in 3x3 AnimalTiles (white, radius 10, 2pt warmBark stroke), round page buttons, paw progress card. With 27 animals there are three page buttons; each is 48pt (AX-1).
  • New sections (About, Top Exhibits) use the same card language: 2pt warmBark outlines, Fredoka headings, Nunito body. Facts are kid-editorial: big number, short label (TC-6). Exhibit cards link to the map with a pin pill, no reading needed beyond the name.
  • Compliance: hours, address and tickets are link-out content, so they sit behind the parental gate in a utility-teal "For grown-ups" card (AR-1; utility pair is the F11 wash, warmBark ink). No mascot there (TC-4b). No third-party links or data on this page (AR-2).
  • Known deviation carried over: the greeting is peachPop text on cream, the same low-contrast pair as the shipped Home (register F1). Not extended anywhere new; the CTA uses warmBark text on peachPop (not white) to avoid adding another F1 pair.
  • Needs Josh: (1) Is "My Places" the right label, or "My Visits"? (2) Should the venue pack page show an exhibit map preview card above Top Exhibits? (3) The exhibit and fact copy is placeholder; it must route through the i18n skill before any build.
