# /zoos/ Design Review, Round 1 (Anna)

Reviewed: zoos.njk, css/zoos.css, js/zoos.js, SPEC v5, 5 screenshots. Contrast figures are computed, not eyeballed. Rule IDs from DESIGN_PRINCIPLES (AX = accessibility, MO = motion, AR/D4CR = compliance and honest commerce); TC-# from the wild-atlas-design taste notes. The system was written for the iOS app, so kid-interaction rules (tap targets of 60pt, mine-sweep safety, parental gate, mascot hosting) do not carry over to a parent-facing web page. I flag where I am relaxing them.

## 1. Verdict
- **Fix first, then preview.** The page is on-brand and clear in structure, and the call-ahead notice is well placed. But the primary orange fails AA on white text, the "unconfirmed" state is nearly invisible on the map, and the List/Map tabs are not keyboard-correct.
- The strongest idea (honest tiers: pins, badges, "Lists this animal" vs unconfirmed) is under-expressed visually. Every card looks equally certain, so the notice has to do all the work.
- Mobile list works well; the controls block is too tall and pushes results below the fold.

## 3. What works well
- The call-ahead notice sits above the results, always visible, with high contrast (9.95:1) and a calm yellow rather than red; it meets the spec requirement and does not scare.
- Outline-only basemap with the Natural Earth credit chip: quiet, on-brand, and puts pins first.
- Cards are clear: photo, name, distance right-aligned in bold, accreditation badge where stars would be, "Show on map" tied to the pin (shot 4 is a good mobile list).
- Typography and palette match the brand: Fredoka headings, Nunito body, cream canvas, rounded 14 to 16px cards, capsule buttons; focus-visible handled everywhere; reduced-motion respected for map movement.
- Honest empty/failure copy ("No problem. We could not use your location...") is non-scolding and keeps the full list available.
