Questions

The quick answers.

Does it cost us anything?

No, it’s free right now. In return, more families get to enjoy Wild Atlas and learn about animals together. We build the first few partner packs at no cost; if we later make a paid product together, we share the revenue.

How do families redeem?

One tap. Each venue gets its own code, redeemed from a link, on the App Store, or in the app’s “Redeem a Code” sheet.

Is it safe for kids and our brand?

No ads, no algorithm, works offline after download, and COPPA compliant. Here’s our Privacy Policy to forward to your team.

Can we make it exclusive to members?

Yes. The per-venue code makes it member-only, with a redemption cap and optional expiry you set.

We already have our own app — does this compete?

No. Wild Atlas is the take-home animal encyclopedia, not a visit-planning or ticketing app. It complements what you run on-site.
