Questions

The quick answers.

Does it cost us anything?

No. Every tier is free to you — you’re giving families a perk, not buying media. Even a custom-built pack or app (Run) is on us to build; if we later turn it into a paid product together, we share the revenue.

How do families redeem?

One tap. Each venue gets a unique code that works from a link, on the App Store, or in the app’s “Redeem a Code” sheet. No instructions to print.

Is it safe for kids and our brand?

Yes — no ads, no algorithm, works fully offline, and COPPA-compliant. It’s the kind of thing an education team can recommend without reputational risk.

Can we make it exclusive to members?

Yes. The per-venue code is what makes an offer member-only, with a redemption cap and optional expiry you set.

We already have our own app — does this compete?

No. Wild Atlas is the take-home animal encyclopedia, not a visit-planning or ticketing app. It complements what you run on-site.
