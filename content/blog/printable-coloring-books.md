## New in the app: Printables

It's called Printables, a new section in Wild Atlas — a printable coloring book for every pack you own. Not clip art: each page starts from the same animal reference the app itself uses, redrawn as line art in a real habitat — a hedgehog in front of a garden wall, not floating on nothing. Three fact bubbles sit around the edge, in the same voice the app narrates in: "Their spines are actually modified hairs made of keratin."

<figure class="page-shot">
  <img src="/assets/blog/printables-hedgehog.png" alt="Wild Atlas coloring page of a hedgehog walking past a stone garden wall, with three fact bubbles around the edges and a QR code in the corner">
  <figcaption>The hedgehog page — real anatomy, a real backdrop, three facts in the app's own voice.</figcaption>
</figure>

Every pack gets a 20-page book like this one: a cover, one page for every animal in the pack, and a closing note. Cozy Critters is one of Wild Atlas's three free packs, so its book is the one you can grab right now — eighteen animals, A4, no purchase, no account, print it at home or take it to a copy shop. Every page carries a QR code to our newsletter, if you'd rather scan than type.

<div class="changelog">
  <h3>Also in 1.0.5</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Packs update themselves.</strong> New content for a pack you own downloads in the background over Wi-Fi, so you're not stuck waiting on a manual refresh before your kid can play. It happens on its own the next time you open the app on a good connection.</li>
    <li><strong>Onboarding is a straight line.</strong> The first-run setup where your child picks a name and an explorer avatar is now a proper step-by-step flow, one thing at a time. Consent is a real, explicit step in that flow too, with no way to decline it by accident.</li>
    <li><strong>The parental gate stops letting you off the hook.</strong> A wrong answer used to dismiss the gate entirely, sending you back to start the whole flow over. Now it just stays up so you can try again immediately.</li>
    <li><strong>Redeem a gift code, and know where.</strong> If someone sends you a code for a pack, the Explorer Pass reveal screen in the Shop now tells you exactly where to redeem it, instead of leaving you to go find the right screen yourself.</li>
    <li><strong>Anonymous usage statistics.</strong> A new toggle in the Grown-ups area of Settings, behind the parental gate — off unless a grown-up turns it on. What it shares, in the app's own words: anonymous counts to Wild Atlas's own servers, things like which packs were opened or whether a download or the audio failed. No names, no identifiers, never third parties. We'd genuinely appreciate you turning it on: without it we can't see which packs are actually getting opened or when something breaks for a family — we're flying blind. It doesn't do anything for your child either way; it just helps us build the next version better.</li>
  </ul>
</div>
