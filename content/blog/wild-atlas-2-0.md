**Wild Atlas 2.0 went live yesterday, and we couldn't be prouder of the new look and feel.**

This is a tour of version 2, and a look at how we built the app.

Version one gave us the app we wanted: an encyclopedia of animal knowledge that kids can drive themselves, at home or on the road. Kids loved it. With 2.0 we've delivered the visual beauty we always dreamed of: living world scenes for every animal, beautiful animation, and a sit-back-and-watch experience, the way you would on safari.

## What's new in 2.0

- **Every pack page and animal page is its own little world.** Scenery that moves, animal visitors passing through, and habitat sounds when your child raises the binoculars. Put them down and the page music returns.
- **Home is a camp with a real sky.** Sun, moon and moon phases, a glow at sunrise and sunset, and a parade of lanterns after dark, all following your phone's time zone.
- **A new look and feel, top to bottom.** New panels, layouts and motion, a new app icon and a starfield launch screen.
- **New profile avatars** drawn in a warm storybook style, and a profile switcher that shows the titles your child has picked up, with a little celebration for each.
- **A redesigned slideshow,** a friendly guide hand in every game, and easier games for our youngest explorers.
- **A polished iPad experience,** with new scenery and layouts.

## A new pack, just in time for Halloween: Super Spiders

Eighteen spiders, from the Goliath birdeater to the peacock spider, each with a baby photo, an in-action shot, size comparisons and narration in six languages. They are a little spooky, which is part of the fun. [Meet Super Spiders](/blog/super-spiders-happy-halloween/). It's $3.99 once, and it's included with the Explorer Pass.

## Fixes and refinements

Who's Who reads answers aloud again for the youngest children, for most animals (English, German, Spanish, French). The trophy total no longer grows by mistake. Jigsaw's Play Again starts a fresh puzzle. Plus many small fixes across scenes, scale pictures, audio and the Home camp.
