  <p>Wild Atlas shipped four updates in July — v1.0.2 through v1.0.4. The big ones: redeem App Store offer codes right inside the app, packs start playing the moment you buy them, and the whole app got a lot more alive with new animations. Free update for everyone.</p>

July was a busy month for Wild Atlas. Four updates shipped in four weeks, and together they add up to a meaningfully different experience — particularly for kids who are just arriving and parents who are handing the app over.

Here's everything that changed, starting with what's newest.

## Redeem an App Store code — right inside the app

Parents who receive a gift code or offer code for Wild Atlas can now redeem it without leaving the app. There's a new Redeem a Code option in the Shop, behind the parental gate. Tap it, enter the code, and the pack unlocks.

This is the path for gifting a pack — if you've been sent a code or received one with a purchase, this is where it goes.

## Pack audio arrives the moment you unlock

Previously, when you bought a pack or redeemed a code, the narration audio didn't start downloading until you reopened the app. That's fixed. Audio now arrives immediately after purchase, redeem, or Family Sharing — no restart needed.

The shop also now shows a download progress indicator while audio is arriving, so you know exactly where things stand.

## New packs float to the top

When a new pack arrives in Wild Atlas, it now appears first — in the Shop and on the home screen. Kids who open the app after an update will see the newest animals without having to scroll.

## Setting up an explorer is now step by step

The first-time setup flow — where a child picks their explorer name and avatar — is now a proper step-by-step sequence: name first, then avatar picker. It's a small change that makes a real difference for younger kids, who can focus on one thing at a time rather than taking in a busier screen all at once.

## Legends of the Wild polish

[Legends of the Wild](/blog/legends-of-the-wild/) launched on July 22 as Wild Atlas's first premium animal pack — 18 rare species, $3.99. This update adds a detail that was missing at launch: the shop preview now narrates in all five non-English languages, so families browsing in German, Spanish, French, Chinese, or British English hear it in their language before they buy.
