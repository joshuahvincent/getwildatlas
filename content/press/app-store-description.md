# App Store Description — Wild Atlas

Raise explorers, not scrollers.

Wild Atlas reconnects children to curiosity instead of numbing it — the screen time you'll actually feel proud of.

Built for ages 3 to 9. No reading required. Every fact is narrated, every screen is child-led, and there is not a single ad, algorithm, or autoplay loop in sight.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 12 animal packs, 200+ creatures from every continent and ocean
• Narrated facts written in plain, child-first language
• Quizzes that grow with your child — multiple difficulty levels
• An interactive world map so they can meet every animal in its home habitat
• Body-size comparisons that put a blue whale next to a school bus and a hummingbird next to their fingertip
• Wild Watch slideshows — Tiny Tails, Sized Up, Weigh to Go, and Wild & Free
• Games Den — four mini-games designed for curiosity, not for engagement metrics

HOW IT WORKS
Three packs are free, forever. The rest unlock through a single parent-approved in-app purchase. Or unlock all nine packs together for $19.99. No subscription. No ads. No data harvesting. One purchase, one app, your child's for life.

DESIGNED FOR REAL CHILDHOODS
Wild Atlas is built like a nature documentary, not a cartoon — calm, cinematic, warm. Your child leads the way. Their curiosity sets the pace. The app stays out of the way so the animals can do what they do best: amaze.

FIVE LANGUAGES AT LAUNCH
English, German, Spanish, French, and Simplified Chinese — fully narrated in every locale.

A NOTE FOR PARENTS
We didn't build Wild Atlas to compete for your child's attention. We built it to return some of it to them — and to the wild, real world waiting outside. Hand it over without the guilt.
