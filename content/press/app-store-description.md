# App Store Description — Wild Atlas

Raise explorers, not scrollers.

Wild Atlas is an app built around your child's curiosity, and screen time you can be proud of. Made for ages 3 to 9. No reading required: every fact is narrated, every screen is child-led, and there is not a single ad, algorithm, or autoplay loop in sight.

Every pack page and animal page is its own little world, with scenery, animal visitors, and habitat sounds when your child raises the binoculars. Home is a camp with a real sky, day and night. New in 2.0: Super Spiders, eighteen hunters, dancers, and silk spinners.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 14 animal packs, 250+ animals from every continent and ocean
• Narrated facts in plain, child-first language
• Quizzes at every level — you pick where they start
• An interactive world map, so they meet every animal in its home habitat
• Body-size comparisons: a blue whale against three school buses, a hummingbird against their thumb
• Wild Watch slideshows — Tiny Tails, Sized Up, Weigh to Go, and Wild & Free
• Game Den — four mini-games designed for curiosity, not for engagement metrics
• A 20-page printable coloring book for every pack
• Narration on for independent play, or off to read together
• Six languages, fully narrated — if your child is learning one, switch it on and they can listen and read in it
• Works offline, so it travels well

HOW IT WORKS
Three packs are free, forever, plus one more of your choice as a welcome gift. The rest unlock with a single parent-approved purchase, or all at once for $23.99. No subscription. No ads. No data harvesting. One purchase, your child's for life.

DESIGNED FOR REAL CHILDHOODS
Built like a nature documentary, not a cartoon: calm, cinematic, warm. Your child leads, their curiosity sets the pace, and the app stays out of the way so the animals can do what they do best: amaze.

We didn't build Wild Atlas to compete for your child's attention. We built it to return some of it to them, and to the wild, real world waiting outside. Hand it over without the guilt.
