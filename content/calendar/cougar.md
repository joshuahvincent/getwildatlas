A cougar has more names than almost any other animal — more than 40 in English alone!

{% figure "/assets/animals/cougar/cover.jpg", "A cougar running across a rocky mountain meadow with tall evergreen trees and snowy peaks behind it" %}

Some people say puma. Some say mountain lion. Some even say panther. They all mean the very same big cat.

Here's a surprise: a cougar can't roar like a lion. Instead, it purrs, meows, and chirps — a little like a house cat does!

Can you purr like a cougar? Take a deep breath and try a soft, rumbly purr.

A cougar's back legs are longer than its front legs, like springs ready to go. They help it make giant leaps — sometimes farther than a car is long. Its long, thick tail swings out behind to help it stay balanced.

{% figure "/assets/animals/cougar/scale.jpg", "A cartoon cougar standing next to a cartoon 5-year-old explorer, showing the cougar's shoulders reach about to the child's chest" %}

A grown-up cougar is about as high as a 5-year-old's chest at the shoulder. It weighs about 70 kilograms (154 pounds) — about the same as four 5-year-olds, all standing on one big scale together!

{% figure "/assets/animals/cougar/weight.jpg", "A scale showing one cougar at 70 kilograms (154 pounds) balanced against four 5-year-olds at 74 kilograms (163 pounds)" %}

Can you picture four friends on one scale?

Cougars are shy and quiet. They move softly, and they are most active at sunrise and sunset, when the light is low. Can you tiptoe as quietly as a cougar?

A baby cougar is called a kitten. It's born with spots and blue eyes! As it grows up, the spots fade away, and its fur turns the soft tan color of its mom.

{% figure "/assets/animals/cougar/baby.jpg", "A spotted baby cougar kitten with pale blue eyes sitting on a mossy rock in a forest" %}

And the Florida panther? It's a cougar, too, living in the swamps and forests of Florida. Today, people are working together to keep these quiet cats safe.
