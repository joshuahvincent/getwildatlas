    Bald eagles are listed as Least Concern, and that is a comeback story. In 1963, only 417 nesting pairs were counted in the lower 48 United States. A chemical called DDT had made eggshells too thin, and many nests failed. People banned DDT, protected eagles and their nesting places, and the birds returned. Bald eagles came off the U.S. endangered species list in 2007, and the most recent federal count, from 2018–2019, estimated about 316,700 bald eagles in the lower 48.

    A small family hope action: fishing line, hooks, and litter near water can tangle birds. Next time you visit a lake or river, bring a bag and pick up anything you find (grown-ups handle the sharp bits). Eagles also do best with quiet space around their nests, so watch from far away with binoculars.

A bald eagle isn't bald at all!

{% figure "/assets/animals/bald_eagle/cover.jpg", "A bald eagle with white head feathers and a yellow beak soaring over a forest and a river", "A bald eagle gliding over its home." %}

"Bald" is an old word that once meant "white-headed." A young eagle has dark feathers on its head. Its white head feathers don't come in until it's about five years old.

Can you flap your arms like wings? An eagle's wings stretch about 2.1 meters (6 feet 11 inches) from tip to tip. That's about twice as wide as a 5-year-old is tall!

{% figure "/assets/animals/bald_eagle/scale.jpg", "A bald eagle with its wings spread wide beside a 5-year-old explorer, with a line marking its 2.1 meter wingspan", "Wings spread wide next to a 5-year-old." %}

Those big wings are made for gliding. And eagles have sharp eyes. A bald eagle can spot a fish swimming from way up high in the sky!

It swoops down and grabs the fish with its feet. Its curved claws hold on tight. Sometimes an eagle lands in the water, and then it can paddle to shore using its wings like oars!

Here's a surprise. For all its size, a bald eagle is light. It weighs about 6 kilograms (13 pounds). Four bald eagles weigh a little more than one 5-year-old!

{% figure "/assets/animals/bald_eagle/weight.jpg", "A scale with four bald eagles on it weighing 24 kilograms, next to a scale with one 5-year-old weighing 19 kilograms", "Four eagles on one side, one 5-year-old on the other." %}

How can something so big weigh so little? Birds have light bones with air inside them, which helps them fly.

Bald eagles build the biggest nests of any bird in North America. They add sticks year after year, and some nests have grown as heavy as a small car! Can you picture that, way up in a tree?

{% figure "/assets/animals/bald_eagle/baby.jpg", "A fluffy gray baby bald eagle sitting in a big nest of sticks beside a calm lake", "A baby eagle in its stick nest." %}

A baby eagle is called an eaglet. It isn't white-headed or dark. It's covered in soft, gray fluff!

One last wow. In movies, the mighty eagle scream you hear is usually a hawk! A real bald eagle's call is a high, squeaky whistle. Can you make a squeaky eagle whistle?

Happy American Eagle Day!
