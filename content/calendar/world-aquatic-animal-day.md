  whatItCelebrates: |
    Aquatic animals live in oceans, rivers, lakes, and ponds. That includes fish, whales and dolphins, octopuses, jellyfish, turtles, and many more.

    This day invites us to notice how many different kinds of animals share the watery parts of our world.
  whyItMatters: |
    Water is home to a huge number of animals, and many people rely on clean water too. When families learn about water animals, pick up litter, and keep waterways tidy, they help make those homes friendlier for everyone.
  funFacts:

    - "Starfish have no brain and no blood."
    - "Dolphins and whales are mammals, so they breathe air."
    - "Clownfish live safely among the waving arms of sea anemones."
    - "The blue whale is the biggest animal known to have ever lived."
  joinIn: |
    Have a water-animal parade. Wiggle like an octopus, glide like a jellyfish, and flap like a fish. Then draw your favourite water animal.

    You can also help any water place by picking up litter near a stream, pond, or beach, with a grown-up.
