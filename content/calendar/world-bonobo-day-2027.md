    Bonobos are listed as Endangered, mostly from bushmeat hunting, habitat loss from logging and farming, and a region where conflict has made conservation harder. There's a hopeful story: since 2002, the sanctuary Lola ya Bonobo, near Kinshasa in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, has cared for orphaned bonobos, and the sanctuary team says about seventy live there today. In 2009, a sanctuary-raised bonobo named Etumbe led the first group ever returned to the wild, into a protected forest reserve called Ekolo ya Bonobo, where the team says about thirty-five bonobos now live free. They keep working to grow the protected land.

    Bonobos need healthy forest with room to live. This week, try checking one label together — a sustainably certified palm oil or FSC-paper product — and talk about why that choice matters. It won't reach the Congo Basin directly, but it's the same idea: forests stay standing when the people who use them look after them.

Deep in the rainforests of the Democratic Republic of the Congo lives an ape that laughs when it's tickled.

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-bonobo-day-2027/cover.jpg", "An adult bonobo swinging through the rainforest canopy on a vine-draped branch" %}

That ape is the bonobo — one of our two closest animal cousins, along with chimpanzees. And just like you, a bonobo really does laugh when someone tickles it, a big panting "hah-hah-hah."

Can you try a tickly bonobo laugh right now?

Here's another surprise. Every single night, a bonobo builds a brand-new bed. It bends leafy branches high up in a tree into a cozy nest — and tomorrow night, it builds a whole new one.

Most of the day, a bonobo walks low on its knuckles and feet, so it looks smaller than it really is. But stand one up tall, next to you...

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-bonobo-day-2027/scale.jpg", "An adult bonobo standing next to a 5-year-old explorer for scale, with a height arrow between them" %}

...and you'd be almost eye to eye.

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-bonobo-day-2027/weight.jpg", "A scale showing an adult bonobo's weight compared to a group of 5-year-old children on a second scale" %}

A grown-up bonobo weighs about as much as two 5-year-olds put together.

Can you knuckle-walk across the floor like a bonobo, hands and feet down low?

In bonobo families, the mums are in charge. A bonobo stays close to its mom its whole life, and her place in the group becomes his place too.

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-bonobo-day-2027/baby.jpg", "A young bonobo sitting on a mossy branch, holding and nibbling a piece of fruit" %}

A baby bonobo rides on its mom, first on her tummy, then on her back, drinking her milk for about four years. Scientists have even seen bonobo moms take care of babies from another group who needed a new family.

Bonobos are famous for making up fast after a squabble, with a hug, some grooming, or sharing a snack. Can you give a make-up hug the next time you bump heads over something?
