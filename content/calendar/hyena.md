Hyenas giggle — and it isn't because someone told a joke!

{% figure "/assets/animals/hyena/cover.jpg", "A spotted hyena running through dry golden grass on an African savanna, kicking up a little dust" %}

A hyena's famous "laugh" is a sound it makes when it's excited or nervous. Hyenas also whoop and growl to talk to their clan.

A clan is a hyena family. Some clans have up to 80 members! And here is a big surprise: the moms are in charge. In a spotted hyena clan, the girls are a little bigger and stronger than the boys.

Can you giggle like a hyena? Can you whoop?

Hyenas are great teammates. They hunt together, and they can run fast, up to 60 kilometers per hour (37 miles per hour). That's about as fast as a car driving through town!

Look closely at a hyena's shape. Its front legs are longer than its back legs, so its back slopes down like a little hill.

{% figure "/assets/animals/hyena/scale.jpg", "A spotted hyena standing next to a 5-year-old explorer, with the hyena's height of 80 centimeters and length of 1.5 meters marked" %}

A hyena stands about 80 centimeters (2 feet 8 inches) tall at the shoulder. That's about as tall as a 5-year-old's chest. Stretched out nose to tail, it's about 1.5 meters (5 feet) long.

A hyena also has one of the strongest bites of any mammal. It can crunch bones the way you crunch a cracker!

{% figure "/assets/animals/hyena/weight.jpg", "A scale showing one hyena at 60 kilograms next to four 5-year-olds at 74 kilograms" %}

A hyena weighs about 60 kilograms (132 pounds). That's about as much as three or four 5-year-olds standing on one scale together!

Can you picture three or four friends on one big scale?

Baby hyenas are called cubs. They're born with their eyes already open, which is unusual for a baby animal. Their fur starts out dark, and spots show up as they grow.

{% figure "/assets/animals/hyena/baby.jpg", "A hyena cub with dark fur and round ears sitting in dry grass at sunset" %}

Hyenas can see very well in the dark, so they're often busy at night. Next time you hear a giggle after sundown, you'll know whose clan it might be!
