    Clouded leopards are listed as Vulnerable. Forests are being cleared, and the cats are also hunted illegally, so numbers have gone down over the last few decades. There's a hopeful story, too: since 2002, a partnership between zoos in the United States and Thailand's Khao Kheow Open Zoo has helped dozens of cubs born in the program survive. The Clouded Leopard Project, started by Point Defiance Zoo staff and volunteers, also supports forest protection and research in Southeast Asia.

    Clouded leopards need standing forest. This week, try choosing paper or wood products with a "responsibly sourced" label, or just spend some time under a tree with your kid. Shhh. Can you hear that? Neither can anyone else. The clouded leopard is one of the quietest, shyest cats there is.

{% figure "/assets/animals/clouded_leopard/cover.jpg", "A clouded leopard with a cloud-patterned coat and a long striped tail walking along a mossy log in a green forest" %}

Look at its coat. It's covered in big, soft, cloud-shaped patches. Every cat's clouds are a little different. In the dappled shade of the forest, those patches help it blend in.

Here's a surprise. Most cats climb up a tree and then back down tail-first. Not this one! A clouded leopard can climb down a tree trunk head-first. Its ankles can turn backwards, like built-in climbing boots.

It can even hang upside down from a branch, holding on with its strong back paws. Can you hang your arms down like a leopard's tail? (Stay on the ground, please!)

Its long tail is almost as long as its body, and it works like a balancing pole when the cat walks along a high, thin branch.

{% figure "/assets/animals/clouded_leopard/scale.jpg", "A clouded leopard about one meter long next to a 5-year-old explorer in a red cape, with arrows marking the cat's 1 meter length and a 56 centimeter height" %}

How big is it? About 1 meter (3 feet 4 inches) from nose to rump, with a tail almost as long again. At the shoulder it stands about 56 centimeters (1 foot 10 inches) tall, about half as tall as a 5-year-old. That's a medium-size cat!

{% figure "/assets/animals/clouded_leopard/weight.jpg", "Two scales: a clouded leopard weighing 18 kilograms and a 5-year-old child weighing 19 kilograms" %}

And it weighs about 18 kilograms (40 pounds), about the same as a 5-year-old. For its size, it has some of the longest teeth of any cat.

Can you stand as still and quiet as a clouded leopard for ten whole seconds?

Baby clouded leopards are called cubs. They learn to climb in the leafy forests of Southeast Asia, where they spend lots of time up high, looking down at the world below.

{% figure "/assets/animals/clouded_leopard/baby.jpg", "A young clouded leopard cub standing on a mossy tree branch in a misty green forest" %}

Clouded leopards purr, hiss, and make a soft, moaning call. But mostly they stay quiet, so they can stay hidden. That's why they're so special to see.
