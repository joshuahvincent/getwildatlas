A turtle wears its skeleton on the outside!

{% figure "/assets/animals/turtle/cover.jpg", "A sea turtle with a brown and orange shell resting on a sandy beach, with trees and the sea in the distance", "A turtle on the beach" %}

A turtle's shell isn't a house it can leave. It's part of the turtle's body, made of many bones joined together. The shell grows as the turtle grows, and it's a bit like a built-in suit of armor.

The shell can feel, too. If you tap it gently, the turtle knows you're there. That's why it's best to watch turtles and not touch them.

Turtles are very, very old. Their family has lived on Earth for more than 200 million years. They were here with the dinosaurs, and they're still here today!

Turtles have no teeth. Instead, they use a strong, sharp beak to nibble and tear their food.

Can you walk like a turtle? Go nice and slow, and tuck your arms in tight!

{% figure "/assets/animals/turtle/scale.jpg", "A small turtle about one foot long on the ground next to a smiling boy in blue pajamas, with an arrow showing the turtle is 30 centimeters long", "A turtle next to a 5-year-old" %}

Turtles come in all sizes. A pet-sized turtle is about 30 centimeters (1 foot) long. A 5-year-old is about 1.1 meters (3 feet 7 inches) tall. You could cuddle one in two hands!

{% figure "/assets/animals/turtle/weight.jpg", "A scale with 37 turtles on one side balanced against one 5-year-old child on the other, both showing about 19 kilograms", "37 turtles weigh about as much as one 5-year-old" %}

A pet-sized turtle weighs about 0.5 kilograms (1 pound). It would take about 37 of them to match one 5-year-old, who weighs about 19 kilograms (41 pounds)!

Some sea turtles are much bigger. The largest turtle ever recorded was a leatherback that weighed over 900 kilograms (about 2,000 pounds). That's as heavy as a small car.

Sea turtles can also hold their breath for a very long time. When they rest, some can stay underwater for hours before they come up for a breath of air.

{% figure "/assets/animals/turtle/baby.jpg", "A tiny baby sea turtle crawling across the sand at dusk, with footprints behind it and the moon shining over the sea", "A baby sea turtle heading for the water" %}

Baby sea turtles hatch on the beach, at night. They head toward the bright, open sky over the sea, where the moon shines on the water. Off they go, flippers scrambling, all the way to the waves!

Can you picture it? A tiny turtle, a big moon, and a whole ocean waiting.
