  grownups: |
    Barn owls are listed as Least Concern worldwide, with millions living on the planet. In some places, though, they're getting harder to find — especially on busy farmland where rough grassy corners and old nesting spots have disappeared, and where rat and mouse poisons and roads can harm them.

    There's a hopeful story: many farmers and wildlife groups put up nest boxes, and barn owls move right in. They're one of the most-loved nest-box birds, and a family of owls can help keep mice in check — a team-up between farmers and wildlife.

    This week's hope action: leave one wild, grassy corner in the yard or park for the small creatures that owls eat, and skip mouse poison if you can — poisoned mice can harm the owls that eat them. (If you have a yard, a nest box for owls is another option; check local advice first.)

Barn owls can find a mouse in the dark using only their ears!

{% figure "/assets/animals/barn_owl/cover.jpg", "A pale barn owl with a heart-shaped face stepping across a grassy field in golden light, with a red barn behind it" %}

Look at that face. It's shaped like a heart! The heart works like a dish. It scoops up tiny sounds and sends them to the owl's ears.

One ear sits a little higher than the other. That helps the owl tell exactly where a sound is coming from. A barn owl can hear a mouse moving under the snow.

Barn owls don't hoot. They screech and hiss instead!

Here's another surprise: barn owls fly in silence. Their feathers are so soft that you can't hear them go by. Sshhh!

Can you flap your arms as quietly as a barn owl?

Their eyes are so big that they can't wiggle them around. So a barn owl turns its whole head instead, almost all the way around to look behind it.

{% figure "/assets/animals/barn_owl/scale.jpg", "A barn owl with its wings spread wide, 95 centimeters across, standing beside a smiling 5-year-old explorer holding a spyglass" %}

A barn owl is not very big. With its wings open, it measures about 95 centimeters (3 feet 2 inches) from tip to tip. That's nearly as wide as a 5-year-old is tall. Stretch out your arms. Who is wider, you or the owl?

{% figure "/assets/animals/barn_owl/weight.jpg", "A scale with 37 barn owls on one side and one 5-year-old on the other, both weighing 19 kilograms" %}

A barn owl weighs about half a kilogram (1 pound), about as much as a loaf of bread. It would take around 37 barn owls to weigh as much as one 5-year-old!

Barn owls have a funny way of finishing a meal. They swallow a mouse whole. Later, they cough up a little fuzzy ball called a pellet. It holds the bones and fur they couldn't use.

Barn owls often live in old barns, church towers, and hollow trees. A baby barn owl is covered in fluffy white down, like a little cotton ball.

{% figure "/assets/animals/barn_owl/baby.jpg", "A fluffy white baby barn owl with a heart-shaped face sitting in straw inside a wooden barn" %}

Barn owls live on every continent except Antarctica. In the farmland, they help by eating mice that nibble on the farmer's grain.

Can you listen very, very quietly for one whole minute? What do you hear?
