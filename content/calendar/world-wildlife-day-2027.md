  grownups: |
    Lions are listed as Vulnerable on the IUCN Red List, with roughly 20,000 to 25,000 left in the wild across Africa, and they've disappeared from about 94% of the land they used to roam — mostly from lost habitat and conflict with people over livestock. There's a hopeful story, too: Rwanda's Akagera National Park lost every one of its lions by 2002. In 2015, conservationists carefully brought seven lions back. By 2023, the park reported more than 50.

    Before your next zoo trip, look up its lion conservation partner together and talk about what "helping lions in the wild" actually means — it's a simple way to turn a visit into something a little more real.

    Starters: "If you were a lion cub, what would you want your pride to be like?" "A lion's roar can travel up to 8 kilometres — how far is that from our house?" "What do you think it takes to bring lions back to a place where they'd disappeared?"

A lion's roar is so loud it can be heard up to 8 kilometres away.

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-wildlife-day-2027/cover.jpg", "A lioness running through golden savanna grass at sunset, kicking up dust" %}

Lions roar to tell their family, "Here I am, this is our home" — and the sound can travel as far as 80 football fields.

Lions are the only big cats that live together in families, called prides. The lion mums and aunties stay together their whole lives, hunting and raising cubs side by side.

Prides make their home on the grassy plains and open woodland of Africa, resting wherever they can find shade.

Can you roar as loud as you can, then whisper it? Which one carries farther?

Stand next to a big lion, and the top of your head wouldn't even reach its shoulder. Nose to tail, a big lion is even longer than two five-year-olds lying down head to toe.

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-wildlife-day-2027/scale.jpg", "A maned lion standing next to a 5-year-old explorer for scale, with arrows marking its length and shoulder height" %}

Only boy lions grow a big, fluffy mane — and it gets darker as they get older.

It would take eleven five-year-olds standing on one big scale to outweigh a single lion!

{% figure "/assets/blog/world-wildlife-day-2027/weight.jpg", "A scale showing a lion's weight compared to eleven 5-year-old explorers on a matching scale" %}

Can you picture eleven friends on one scale, all trying to balance out one lion?

Lions spend most of the day resting in the shade — up to 20 hours!

Can you curl up small like a hiding cub, then stretch up tall like a grown lion?

Baby lions are called cubs. They're born with spots on their fur that fade as they grow, and their eyes stay shut for about their first week or two. Mum keeps them hidden and safe for a few weeks before they meet the rest of the pride.
