    House sparrows are small brown and grey birds that often live close to people, in gardens, streets, and old walls. They chirp, hop, and splash in puddles.

  whyItMatters: |

  funFacts:

    - "Male house sparrows have a dark bib under the beak."
    - "Sparrows often live in groups and chatter together."
    - "They take dust baths to keep their feathers tidy."
    - "Sparrows like to nest in little holes and tucked-away corners."
