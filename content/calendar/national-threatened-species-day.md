    A threatened species is an animal or plant that needs extra care so it can keep living in the wild. This day is a chance to learn about them and about the people working to protect them.

    The thylacine, or Tasmanian tiger, was a striped animal from Tasmania. Remembering it helps us look after others.
  whyItMatters: |
    Every animal has a place in its home. The good news is that people are working hard for many species, and families can help too. Learning about a threatened animal and sharing what you learn is a lovely first step.
  funFacts:

    - "Tasmanian devils still live in the wild in Tasmania."
    - "Numbats are small striped Australian animals that eat termites, thousands a day."
    - "Greater bilbies dig burrows with their strong front paws."
