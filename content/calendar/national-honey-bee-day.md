  whatItCelebrates: |
    This day celebrates honey bees and the people who look after them. Honey bees live together in big, busy families called colonies, and everyone has a job to do.

    As bees visit flower after flower looking for sweet nectar, they carry a dusting of pollen along with them. That helps many plants make seeds and fruit.
  whyItMatters: |
    Bees help flowers, fruits and vegetables grow, and that is good for lots of other animals too. The good news is that families can help. Planting flowers and leaving a little wild corner in the garden gives bees a place to snack.
  funFacts:
    - "A honey bee's wings beat about 200 times every second. That is what makes the buzzing sound!"
    - "Honey bees can tell their hive-mates where the flowers are by doing a wiggly \"waggle dance.\""
    - "A honey bee has five eyes: two big ones and three tiny ones on top of its head."
    - "One worker bee makes only about a twelfth of a teaspoon of honey in her whole life."
    - "Honey bees carry pollen on their back legs, in little pollen \"baskets.\""
  joinIn: |
    Plant something flowery in a pot, a garden or a window box, and watch who visits. Bees like to keep busy, so give them space and watch from a distance. A shallow dish of water with pebbles in it makes a handy drinking spot. Ask a grown-up to help.
