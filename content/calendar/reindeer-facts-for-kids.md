    Reindeer and caribou are listed as Vulnerable, and migratory tundra caribou herds have fallen by about 65% over the past two to three decades — mostly from a warming Arctic and habitat loss. There's a hopeful story too: the European wild forest reindeer had nearly disappeared from Finland's forests, and zoos including Helsinki's Korkeasaari Zoo raised reindeer that went on to help start new wild herds. More than 80 reindeer have been released into national parks where forest reindeer had been missing, and a new project is already under way to keep the recovery going.

    Reindeer need cold, snowy homes to stay healthy. This week, try turning off the lights when you leave a room, or take a walk instead of a car ride. It won't change the Arctic on its own, but it's the same idea: small, everyday choices add up to a kinder world for the animals that need it to stay cold.

Reindeer are the only deer in the world where the girls grow antlers too!

{% figure "/assets/blog/reindeer-facts-for-kids/cover.jpg", "A reindeer bounding across the snowy tundra" %}

Boy and girl reindeer both grow antlers — and a big male's antlers can stretch longer than you are tall. Many reindeer mums keep theirs all winter long, right up until their calves are born in spring.

Deep inside a reindeer's eyes is a hidden mirror. It's gold in summer and turns deep blue once winter comes, which helps a reindeer see through the long, dark Arctic night.

Listen closely and you might hear them coming before you see them — reindeer feet go click, click, click as they walk. Scientists think the sound may help a herd stay together in fog or snow.

Can you click your tongue like a reindeer's feet?

Their big, wide hooves work like snowshoes on snow, and like paddles when they swim across rivers.

Can you stomp your feet wide, like a reindeer crossing deep snow?

{% figure "/assets/blog/reindeer-facts-for-kids/scale.jpg", "A reindeer standing beside a 5-year-old explorer for scale" %}

A big reindeer stands taller than you at the shoulder — and nose to tail, it's almost twice as long as you are tall.

{% figure "/assets/blog/reindeer-facts-for-kids/weight.jpg", "A reindeer on a scale balanced against nine 5-year-olds" %}

A grown-up reindeer weighs about as much as eight 5-year-olds put together! Can you count all nine friends on this big scale?

{% figure "/assets/blog/reindeer-facts-for-kids/baby.jpg", "A reindeer calf standing alone on the spring tundra" %}

A newborn reindeer calf weighs about as much as a big bag of flour — and it can double its weight in about two weeks. It stands up within an hour of being born, and by the very next day, it can run faster than a person.

That's the real reindeer — quietly incredible, long before any sleigh bells ring.
