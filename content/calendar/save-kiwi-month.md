    Kiwi are special birds found only in New Zealand. They cannot fly, so they spend their nights walking through the forest, poking their long beaks into the soil to find yummy worms and bugs.

    - "Kiwi feathers look and feel more like shaggy hair than bird feathers."
    - "A kiwi lays an egg that is very big for its small body."
