  whatItCelebrates: |
    Curlews are shorebirds with very long, curved bills and speckled brown feathers. They live on moors, meadows, marshes and beaches.

    World Curlew Day is a day to listen for their calls and to help people notice these wonderful wading birds.
  whyItMatters: |
    Curlews need wide, quiet places to nest, like wet meadows and moorland. When farmers, neighbours and nature groups care for those places, curlew families have room to raise their chicks. Every little bit of listening and learning helps.
  funFacts:
    - "A curlew's bill curves downward, which is perfect for probing into soft mud."
    - "The Eurasian curlew has one of the longest bills of any shorebird in Europe."
    - "Its call sounds like it is saying its own name, \"cur-lee!\""
    - "Curlews nest on the ground, and their speckled feathers help them blend in."
    - "Curlew chicks are fluffy and can walk soon after hatching."
  joinIn: |
