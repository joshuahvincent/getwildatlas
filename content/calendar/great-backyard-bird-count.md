  whatItCelebrates: |
    The Great Backyard Bird Count is a citizen-science event, where ordinary people help scientists by noticing and counting birds. It lasts a few days in mid-February, and you can join for as little as 15 minutes.

    You can watch from your yard, a park, a school playground, or right through a window.
  whyItMatters: |
    Birds travel and change places through the year, and no scientist can watch everywhere at once. When many people count in the same few days, the pictures add up to a big map of where birds are. Your short watch can be one small, happy piece of it.

  joinIn: |
    It's free and open to anyone, including beginners. Check the Bird Count website below for this year's dates and how to take part, then watch for at least 15 minutes from your own yard, a park, or a window.

    Jot down each kind of bird and how many you see at once. Ask a grown-up to help if you'd like to share your list online.
