  whatItCelebrates: |
    The dawn chorus is the burst of birdsong around sunrise, when many birds sing at once. It is loudest in spring.

    Birds sing to share who they are, mark out their home patch and call to a mate. Together it sounds like music made by the whole neighbourhood.
  whyItMatters: |
    Listening to birds helps us notice who lives near us. When we notice, we care, and families can make little changes, like planting flowers or leaving out fresh water, that make gardens and parks friendlier for birds.
  funFacts:
    - "Birdsong is usually loudest around sunrise."
    - "Birds make sound with a special voice box called a syrinx."
    - "Many songbirds sing to say, \"This is my patch!\""
    - "In Europe, the robin is often one of the first birds to start singing."
    - "Birds can learn songs. Young songbirds listen and practise to get theirs right."
  joinIn: |
    Pick a morning and, with a grown-up, open a window or step outside near sunrise. Listen for five minutes and count how many different songs you hear. It is free and works from your own yard, balcony or park. Keep voices soft, and later try making up your own bird songs.
