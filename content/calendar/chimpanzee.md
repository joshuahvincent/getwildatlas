Chimpanzees make their own tools — and they use them to go fishing for snacks!

{% figure "/assets/animals/chimpanzee/cover.jpg", "A black-furred chimpanzee walking through a sunlit forest, holding a small stick in one hand in a dim, leafy forest" %}

Here's how it works. A chimpanzee finds a thin stick and pokes it into a tiny hole in a termite mound. The termites grab on. Then out comes the stick, and the chimp licks them off. Yum!

Chimpanzees crack nuts, too. They put a nut on a flat stone and bang it with another rock, like a little hammer. Bang, bang, bang!

When a chimp is thirsty, it can chew up a bunch of leaves to make a sponge. It dips the sponge in water and then squeezes it into its mouth.

Can you pretend to be a chimp using a stick? What would you fish for?

{% figure "/assets/animals/chimpanzee/scale.jpg", "A chimpanzee standing next to a 5-year-old explorer for scale, with a height marker showing 1.2 meters (4 feet)" %}

A chimpanzee stands about 1.2 meters (4 feet) tall. That's a little taller than a 5-year-old, who stands about 109 centimeters (3 feet 7 inches).

{% figure "/assets/animals/chimpanzee/weight.jpg", "Two scales: one chimpanzee at 50 kilograms (110 pounds) next to three 5-year-old explorers at 56 kilograms (122 pounds)" %}

A chimpanzee weighs about 50 kilograms (110 pounds). That's almost the same as three 5-year-olds standing on one big scale together!

Chimpanzees are very good at being a family. They greet each other with hugs and kisses, and they pat each other on the back to say "it's okay." Every chimp even has its own fingerprints, just like you.

At bedtime, a chimpanzee doesn't go home. It builds a brand-new nest of leafy branches high up in a tree, and it makes a fresh one every night.

{% figure "/assets/animals/chimpanzee/baby.jpg", "A baby chimpanzee sitting on a mossy log in a sunny green forest" %}

Baby chimpanzees ride on their mom's back, a little like a piggyback ride, for years. They watch everything their mom does, and that's how they learn to use tools.

Chimpanzees talk with hoots, screams, and pant-laughs when they play. Can you do a chimp hoot? Oooh-oooh-ooh-AH-AH! Now can you laugh like a chimp, with lots of panting?
