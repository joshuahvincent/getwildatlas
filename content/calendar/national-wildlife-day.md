  whatItCelebrates: |
    National Wildlife Day is a day to notice and enjoy the wild animals we share the world with. That includes the squirrel in the park, the bird at the window and the whale far out at sea.

    It is also a day to remember Steve Irwin, who shared his big love for animals with people all over the world.
  whyItMatters: |
    Every wild animal has a part to play, from bees that visit flowers to whales that travel across oceans. When we get curious about them, we take better care of the places they live. Even a small thing, like putting out water for the birds, is a kind way to help.

    - "An octopus has three hearts."
    - "African elephants use their trunks to drink, smell, hug and pick things up."
    - "Many animals, like owls, come out at night while we are asleep."
  joinIn: |
