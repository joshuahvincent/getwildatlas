  grownups: |
    Arctic wolves are a subspecies of the gray wolf, which the IUCN lists as Least Concern; the Arctic wolf itself isn't assessed separately. They live so far north that few people share their home, so they face fewer pressures than many wolves farther south. Scientists are still watching how a warming Arctic may change the tundra and the animals the wolves depend on.

    A hopeful story: in 2025, Edmonton Valley Zoo opened a much larger Arctic wolf habitat, and in 2024 Nasu Animal Kingdom in Japan welcomed the country's first Arctic wolf pups. Zoos like these help people meet wolves up close and care about them.

    One family hope action: wolves and the animals they hunt depend on healthy, wild places. This week, try walking or biking one short trip instead of riding in a car, and talk about how that helps keep wild places cool.

Arctic wolves have fur on the bottoms of their paws!

{% figure "/assets/animals/arctic_wolf/cover.jpg", "A white Arctic wolf trotting across a wide, flat field of snow" %}

Fur on their feet works like cozy socks. It keeps their paws warm on the ice and snow.

Arctic wolves live in the far north, in Canada and Greenland. It is one of the coldest, snowiest places on Earth. Their thick double coat is so warm they can curl up and nap outside in a snowstorm. Their ears are small and their noses are short, which helps keep the cold out too.

And their white coat? It helps them blend in with the snow.

Here is a wow fact. In summer up there, the sun doesn't set for months. Arctic wolves can hunt in daylight all day and all night!

Wolves are team players. A pack works together to find food, and it can travel a very long way in a single day. That's like walking from your town to the next town over.

Can you tiptoe like a wolf across the snow?

{% figure "/assets/animals/arctic_wolf/scale.jpg", "An Arctic wolf standing next to a 5-year-old explorer for scale, with the wolf's shoulder height and body length marked" %}

How big is an Arctic wolf? A big one is about 80 centimeters (2 feet 8 inches) tall at the shoulder. That's about as high as your chest. From nose to tail tip, it's about 1.6 meters (5 feet 3 inches) long.

{% figure "/assets/animals/arctic_wolf/weight.jpg", "A scale showing one Arctic wolf at 45 kilograms next to three 5-year-olds at 56 kilograms" %}

A big Arctic wolf weighs about 45 kilograms (99 pounds). That's about as much as two or three five-year-olds standing on one scale!

Wolves talk to each other with a howl. It can ring out long and loud across the snowy land. Can you howl like a wolf? Go ahead. Aaa-oooooo!

{% figure "/assets/animals/arctic_wolf/baby.jpg", "A fluffy white Arctic wolf pup lying on mossy tundra with snowy hills behind it" %}

Baby wolves are called pups. They start out tiny, with their eyes closed, and they grow up with their pack close by.

Now you know the wolf with the warm, furry paws.
