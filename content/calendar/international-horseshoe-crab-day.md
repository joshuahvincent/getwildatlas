  whatItCelebrates: |
    This day celebrates horseshoe crabs, sea animals with a hard, curved shell and a long pointed tail. Each spring and summer, some kinds come up on beaches to lay eggs.

    The day is also about the coasts where they live.
  whyItMatters: |
    Horseshoe crabs are very old animals, and healthy beaches help them and the birds and other creatures that visit those beaches. Keeping beaches clean and calm is a good way to help.
  funFacts:
    - "Horseshoe crabs have been around for hundreds of millions of years, much longer than the dinosaurs."
    - "Even though the name says crab, they are more closely related to spiders."
    - "The long tail helps a horseshoe crab turn itself back over if it gets flipped."
    - "The tail is not a stinger, and a horseshoe crab does not bite."
    - "Their eggs are food for many beach birds."
  joinIn: |
