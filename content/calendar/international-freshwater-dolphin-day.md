  whatItCelebrates: |
    River dolphins live in big, winding rivers in places like South America and Asia. The Amazon river dolphin is sometimes pink, and the Ganges river dolphin has a long, thin snout.

    This day shines a light on these clever swimmers and their muddy, wonderful homes.
  whyItMatters: |
    Rivers are home to dolphins, fish and the people who live beside them. When communities, scientists and families care for clean, healthy rivers, river dolphins have room to swim and play.
  funFacts:
    - "River dolphins live in fresh water, not salty seawater."
    - "The Amazon river dolphin is called the boto, and grown-up botos can look pink."
    - "Ganges river dolphins use clicking sounds to find their way in muddy water."
    - "The Ganges river dolphin is India's national aquatic animal."
    - "River dolphins can turn their heads, which helps them steer around tree roots."
