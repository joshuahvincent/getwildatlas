Shh... listen closely. Can you hear an Amur tiger? No? That's because it's walking on soft, padded paws, and it makes almost no sound at all!

{% figure "/assets/animals/amur_tiger/cover.jpg", "An Amur tiger with orange and black stripes walking through deep snow in a birch forest" %}

The Amur tiger is one of the biggest cats in the whole world. It lives in the cold, snowy forests of far-eastern Russia. A thick, furry coat keeps it warm and cosy, even when snow is falling all around.

Here's a secret about those stripes. No two tigers have the same pattern. Your fingerprints are all your own, and so are a tiger's stripes! Even the skin under its fur is striped.

Can you trace a pretend stripe on your arm?

{% figure "/assets/animals/amur_tiger/scale.jpg", "An Amur tiger shown next to a 5-year-old explorer, with measurements for the tiger's length" %}

A big Amur tiger can be about 3 meters (almost 10 feet) long from nose to tail. That's longer than almost three 5-year-olds lying head to toe!

An Amur tiger can weigh about 200 kilograms (441 pounds). That's about as much as 11 five-year-olds standing together on one big scale.

{% figure "/assets/animals/amur_tiger/weight.jpg", "A scale showing one Amur tiger at 200 kilograms balanced against 11 five-year-olds" %}

Can you picture 11 friends on one scale?

Tigers also love water. The Amur tiger is a strong swimmer, even in chilly rivers. Most cats don't like getting wet, but not this one!

And what does a tiger sound like? Far away, it can let out a deep, rumbling roar that booms through the trees. Up close and friendly, it makes a soft, puffing sound called a chuff.

Can you try a gentle chuff? Puff your cheeks and blow out softly.

Tiger cubs are born tiny. They stay close to their mom, learning how to creep through the forest, until they are about two years old.

{% figure "/assets/animals/amur_tiger/baby.jpg", "An Amur tiger cub looking out from behind a snowy fallen log in a forest" %}

Now you know the Amur tiger's secrets: silent paws, fingerprint stripes, and a love of snowy swims.
