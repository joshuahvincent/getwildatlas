  grownups: |
    Red pandas are listed as Endangered by the IUCN, mostly because of habitat loss as forests are cleared, along with hunting and trapping. Fewer than 10,000 are thought to remain across India, Bhutan, China, Nepal, and Myanmar. There's a hopeful story from Nepal: in 2010, the Red Panda Network started a "Forest Guardians" program with just 16 local members, who walk the forest to monitor red pandas and look after their habitat. Today the Network works with community partners and dozens of community forests, and the guardians' work has helped bring poaching in their project areas down to almost zero. Local people protecting the forest they live beside is one of the biggest reasons for hope.

    A family hope action: choose paper and wood products from forests that are looked after well (look for a forest-stewardship label), and talk together about why forests matter to animals like red pandas.

Psst! Look up, up, up in the trees. Do you see a fuzzy red-orange face peeking out? That's a red panda!

{% figure "/assets/animals/red_panda/cover.jpg", "A red panda leaping along a mossy branch in a misty mountain forest, its long ringed tail stretched out behind" %}

Red pandas live in cool mountain forests in Asia. They are wonderful climbers. They sleep in the trees all day and wake up around dusk to look for food.

Here's a cozy secret. When it's cold, a red panda wraps its long, bushy, striped tail around itself like a blanket. A built-in blanket!

Can you wrap your arms around yourself like a red panda curled up for a nap?

Red pandas aren't close cousins of giant pandas at all. They have their own special animal family, all by themselves.

{% figure "/assets/animals/red_panda/scale.jpg", "A red panda standing next to a 5-year-old explorer, with measurements showing the panda's length and height" %}

A red panda is small. It is about 65 centimeters (2 feet 2 inches) long, and it stands about 29 centimeters (1 foot) tall at the shoulder. That's about as high as your knee!

A red panda weighs about 5 kilograms (11 pounds). That's about the same as a house cat. It takes about four red pandas to weigh as much as one 5-year-old.

{% figure "/assets/animals/red_panda/weight.jpg", "A scale showing four red pandas at 20 kilograms next to one 5-year-old child at 19 kilograms" %}

Can you picture four red pandas on one scale?

Red pandas munch mostly bamboo. To hold it tight, they have a special wrist bone that works a bit like a thumb. Hold up your hand. A red panda has an extra one, kind of!

And here's a trick: a red panda can walk down a tree headfirst. Its back ankles can twist right around. Can you wiggle your ankles and try?

How do red pandas talk to each other? With squeaks, chirps, and little whistles. Can you squeak like a red panda?

Red panda cubs are tiny when they're born. They stay snug in a cozy nest with their mom, and they stay close to her for about a year.

{% figure "/assets/animals/red_panda/baby.jpg", "A young red panda with white-edged ears and a white face sitting on a mossy forest floor, its striped tail curled beside it" %}

Now you know the red panda's secrets: a blanket tail, a false thumb, and a headfirst climb down.
