A raccoon sees the world with its hands!

{% figure "/assets/animals/raccoon/cover.jpg", "A raccoon with a black mask and a ringed tail walking along a log in a park" %}

Raccoons wear a black mask, like a little bandit. And their front paws are super clever. The paws are packed with feeling, and they get even more sensitive when they're wet. So a raccoon dips its food in water to feel it better. It looks like washing. It's really feeling!

Can you wiggle your fingers like a raccoon? Pretend you're feeling something in a stream. Is it smooth? Is it bumpy?

{% figure "/assets/animals/raccoon/scale.jpg", "A raccoon about 30 centimetres tall, standing beside a 5-year-old child for scale" %}

A raccoon is small. On all fours, it comes up to about 30 centimetres (1 foot) at the shoulder. That's about as high as your knee. It's around 70 centimetres (2 feet 4 inches) long, with a ringed tail.

A raccoon weighs about 8 kilograms (18 pounds). That's about as much as a bowling ball. Three raccoons together would weigh a bit more than one 5-year-old.

{% figure "/assets/animals/raccoon/weight.jpg", "Three raccoons on a scale next to one 5-year-old child on another scale" %}

Raccoons are great climbers. Their back feet can turn around, so they can climb down a tree headfirst. Their bushy tails help them balance up high.

They're clever, too. Raccoons can open jars and unlatch gates. They can remember how to solve a problem for a long time.

Can you picture a raccoon turning a latch? What would it be trying to reach?

Raccoons are fast when they need to be. They can run about 24 kilometres per hour (15 miles per hour). They can swim, too, when they need to.

A baby raccoon is called a kit. Kits stay with their mom for about a year.

{% figure "/assets/animals/raccoon/baby.jpg", "A baby raccoon kit with a black mask standing on a rock in a forest stream" %}

Raccoons will eat almost anything: fruit, nuts, bugs, and fish. At night they chitter and purr when they're happy.

Can you make a raccoon chitter? Say it softly, like a tiny, happy engine.
