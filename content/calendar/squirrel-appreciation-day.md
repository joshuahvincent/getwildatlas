    Squirrels live in parks, yards, and forests nearly everywhere. They are always on the move, burying nuts and seeds, scampering along branches, and leaping from tree to tree.

    This day is for noticing and enjoying these everyday wild neighbors.
  whyItMatters: |
    Squirrels are part of the neighborhood nature all around us. Getting to know them helps kids see that wildlife isn't just far away. It's right outside, too. Watching quietly from a distance is a lovely way to be a friend to a wild animal.
  funFacts:
    - "A squirrel's front teeth never stop growing, so chewing keeps them just the right size."
    - "Squirrels bury food for later, and they don't always find every nut. Forgotten ones can sprout into new trees."
    - "A squirrel's bushy tail helps it balance on thin branches."
    - "Squirrels can turn their back feet around so they can climb down a tree headfirst."
    - "Squirrels use their sharp sense of smell to sniff out buried nuts."
  joinIn: |
    Take a squirrel-spotting walk. Watch quietly from a distance and see what the squirrel does. Does it bury something? Flick its tail? Try drawing it from memory afterward.

    Wild squirrels do best finding their own food, so enjoy them from afar and leave the snacks to nature.
