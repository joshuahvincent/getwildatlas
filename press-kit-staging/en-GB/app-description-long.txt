Wild Atlas is an interactive animal discovery app for children aged 3 to 9. Screen time you'll feel proud of. No reading required.

Every single fact is narrated aloud — which means a three-year-old can move through the app entirely on their own. Content is calm, cinematic, and warm. It's built like a nature documentary, not a cartoon. The child leads. The app stays out of the way.

Inside, children find 200+ animals across 12 thematic packs covering every continent and ocean. Facts are written in plain, child-first language and delivered at a pace kids can follow. Quizzes scale with the child — picture-based for younger children, reading-based for older ones, with multiple difficulty levels throughout. An interactive world map lets children meet every animal in its real home habitat.

Wild Atlas goes further than lists of facts. Body-size comparisons put a blue whale next to a double-decker bus and a hummingbird next to a fingertip — grounding the extraordinary in the everyday. Wild Watch slideshows (Tiny Tails, Sized Up, Weigh to Go, Wild & Free) offer curated thematic explorations. The Games Den includes four mini-games — Memory Match, Puppy Puzzle, Who's Who, and Habitat Hop — designed for curiosity, not for engagement metrics. The app works fully offline.

A hidden pack — the Mythical Menagerie — can be unlocked by discovering shooting stars hidden throughout the app.

Three packs are free forever: Happy Hounds, Cool Cats, and Cozy Critters. Additional packs are £3.99 each as one-time, parent-approved purchases. All ten paid packs are available together as the Explorer Pass.

No subscription. No ads. No data harvesting. No autoplay. COPPA-compliant. Available at launch in six languages — English (US and UK editions), German, Spanish, French, and Simplified Chinese — fully narrated in every one.

Wild Atlas is independently built and funded by Joshuah Vincent, a Seattle-based product builder and father of two. It is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
