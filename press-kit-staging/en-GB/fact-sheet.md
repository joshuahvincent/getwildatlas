## Content

- **200+ animals** across **12 packs** covering every continent and ocean
- **3 free packs at launch:** Happy Hounds, Cool Cats, Cozy Critters
- **9 paid packs** (individual or via Explorer Pass bundle)
- **Hidden bonus pack:** Mythical Menagerie (18 creatures from world folklore — unlock by finding shooting stars hidden throughout the app)

### Features

- Fully narrated facts — no reading required; suitable from age 3
- Dual-mode quizzes: picture-based (younger children) and reading-based (older children), multiple difficulty levels
- Interactive world map — every animal placed in its real habitat
- Body-size and weight comparisons
- Wild Watch slideshows: Tiny Tails, Sized Up, Weigh to Go, Wild & Free
- Games Den: Memory Match, Puppy Puzzle, Who's Who, Habitat Hop (4 mini-games)
- Full offline support

### Languages

English (US), English (UK), German, Spanish, French, Simplified Chinese — fully narrated in every language.
