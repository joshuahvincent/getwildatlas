## Wild Atlas, a Narrated Animal Discovery App for Children, Launches Globally in Five Languages

*Built by the son of South Africa's celebrated conservation documentary filmmakers, Wild Atlas gives children documentary-style animal content — and parents screen time they can hand over without worry.*

**SEATTLE, WA — [LAUNCH DATE]** — Wild Atlas, an interactive animal discovery app for children aged 3 and up, is available today on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Across twelve animal packs and five languages, the app brings narrated, documentary-style facts, games, quizzes, and exploration to young children — with no subscription, no advertising, and no algorithmic feed.

Wild Atlas is the work of Joshuah Vincent, a Seattle-based product builder and the son of Michael and Sharyn Vincent, the founders of Jasmin Films — a South African production company that spent four decades documenting the coelacanth, great white sharks, black eagles, and the elephants of Addo. Michael passed away in March 2024. The following year, Joshuah led a three-week overlanding expedition through Botswana's game reserves — partly with money his father left him, partly in memory of trips they never got to take together. Wild Atlas was built in the months that followed.

*"My parents spent their lives in the bush, making it accessible to people through film. I grew up on those shoots, in the South African veld, learning about animals and why they're worth knowing. I wanted screen time that my wife and I could hand to the kids without worrying — something educational and delightful, with no ads or junk food video. I couldn't find it, and we looked hard. So we built it."*
— Joshuah Vincent, founder, Wild Atlas

The app launches with twelve packs across five languages — English (US and UK editions), German, Spanish, French, and Simplified Chinese. Three packs are free at launch: Happy Hounds, Cool Cats, and Cozy Critters. Remaining packs are £3.99 each as one-time, parent-approved purchases, or all nine can be unlocked together for £19.99. Each pack includes narrated facts, dual-mode quizzes, an interactive world map, body-size comparisons, and animal sounds. Four games — Memory Match, Puppy Puzzle, Who's Who, and Habitat Hop — are included across all packs. The app works fully offline — so kids can enjoy it on aeroplanes, long journeys, or anywhere without reliable internet.

A hidden pack — the Mythical Menagerie, featuring eighteen creatures from world folklore — can be unlocked by finding the hidden shooting stars triggered throughout the app over multiple sessions.

In early testing, an eight-year-old who "hardly uses devices in general" tried Wild Atlas and had one word: *"Addictive."*

Wild Atlas is COPPA-compliant, collects no personal data, and is independently built and funded.
