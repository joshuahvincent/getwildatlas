## 80 WORDS — for press "About" sections

Joshuah Vincent is the founder of Wild Atlas, a narrated animal discovery app for children aged 3 and up. A Senior Principal Product Manager at Meta for six years — shipping AR experiences including Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and Orion, Meta's next-generation AR platform — he previously led product at Zillow and Xero, and holds 25+ patents across AR, AI, and 3D visualisation. The son of South African conservation documentary filmmakers, he grew up in the veld learning about animals from his parents. He lives in Seattle with his wife and two young children.
