## 80 WORDS — for press "About" sections

Joshuah Vincent is the founder of Wild Atlas — a narrated animal discovery app for children, independently built and launched in June 2026. The son of South African conservation documentary filmmakers Michael and Sharyn Vincent (Jasmin Films), he grew up in the veld learning about animals from his parents. He spent six years at Meta as a Principal Product Manager, shipping AR experiences including Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and Orion. He holds 25+ patents across AR, AI, and 3D visualisation. He lives in Seattle with his wife and two young children.
