Wild Atlas is the animal app parents don't feel guilty handing to their kids.

It's built for ages 3 to 9 — and because no reading is required, even a three-year-old can use it independently. Every fact is narrated aloud. Every screen is child-led. There are 200+ animals across 13 packs, an interactive world map, body-size comparisons, Wild Watch slideshows, and a Games Den with four mini-games. All of it feels like a nature documentary, not an app.

Three packs — Happy Hounds, Cool Cats, and Cozy Critters — are free forever. Additional packs are $3.99 each (one-time, parent-approved). All ten paid packs together are $23.99 via the Explorer Pass.

No subscription. No ads. No algorithms. No data harvesting. COPPA-compliant. Available in English, German, Spanish, French, and Simplified Chinese.

Raise explorers, not scrollers.
